🍉 Fruit Merge – Cute Fruits Evolution Game

Drop • Merge • Grow • Discover!

Fruit Merge is a colourful, Suika-style fruit merging puzzle game featuring cute cartoon fruits, expressive faces, bright fruit colours, smooth physics, and satisfying merge animations.

🎮 Features

- 🍒 Fruit Evolution: Merge two identical fruits to create a bigger fruit.
- 🍎 Cute Cartoon Design: Colourful fruits with expressive eyes, smiles, and adorable reactions.
- 🍇 Smooth Physics: Fruits fall, collide, roll, and bounce naturally.
- 🍊 Growing Challenge: Progress through fruit levels and aim to create the ultimate watermelon.
- 🔊 Sound Effects: Enjoy satisfying sounds when fruits drop, collide, and merge.
- 🎵 Music Controls: Turn game music and sound effects on or off in Settings.
- 📱 Mobile-Friendly: Play on smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers.
- 🏆 High Score: Track your best score and try to beat your record.
- 🧩 Strategic Gameplay: Position fruits carefully to create bigger combinations and avoid overflowing the container.

🕹️ How to Play

1. Move the fruit left or right.
2. Tap or release to drop it into the container.
3. Merge two matching fruits to create the next fruit in the evolution chain.
4. Keep merging, collect points, and discover larger fruits.
5. Prevent the fruits from overflowing the container.
6. Try to create the biggest fruit: Watermelon! 🍉
